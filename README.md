## Hi, I'm Nidhi

I work with ML across a wider range of domains than most people find sensible — protein binding prediction, SARS-CoV-2 variant calling, ophthalmology Q&A, voice cloning, music generation. The common thread is taking large pretrained models and fine-tuning or adapting them for something specific, usually without a cloud bill.

## What's in here

Projects tend to cluster around a few areas:

- **Biomedical ML** — ESM2 + QLoRA for protein binding-site prediction, ESMFold, drug synergy modeling, COVID variant calling
- **LLMs & RAG** — local Mistral-7B for medical Q&A (ophthalmology), PotterGPT, RAGAS evaluation pipelines
- **Audio AI** — real-time voice cloning, TTS, fine-tuning ACE-Step 1.5 for music generation
- **Vision** — Meta's SAM for segmentation, federated learning on image classifiers

Most things are Python, run locally, and involve some flavor of LoRA or quantization to fit on consumer hardware.
